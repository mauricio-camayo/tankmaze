TankMaze Game Days

TankMaze matches only run during a scheduled Game Day: a two-phase tournament. Every registered tank first plays a round-robin group of roughly eight tanks, then the top finishers move into a single-elimination bracket down to a champion.

Round robin

Registered tanks are ranked by Global Score and split into groups of about eight, seeded so each group gets an even spread of stronger and weaker tanks. Every tank in a group plays every other tank once; a win is worth 1 point, a flawless win (no damage taken) is worth 2.

Elimination bracket

The top finishers from each group advance into a single-elimination bracket seeded best-vs-worst. Lose once and you're out — the last tank standing is the Game Day Champion.

Recurring series

Some Game Days repeat on a schedule (weekly, monthly, or every N days) — each occurrence still runs and scores independently, and results feed straight into every registered tank's spot on the global leaderboard.
