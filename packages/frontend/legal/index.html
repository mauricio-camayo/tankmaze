Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Privacy Policy

TankMaze operates the game platform at tankmaze.org. When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and (for Google, GitHub, or Discord sign-in) a profile picture provided by that identity provider — we never receive your password on any of them. We also store the tank code and compiled WASM binaries you submit, your match results, your subscription tier, and any avatar image you upload for a tank. If you use Friends & direct messaging, we store friend relationships and message content, which is automatically deleted 30 days after being sent.

Free-tier accounts see Google AdSense ads, which use cookies for ad personalization; Builder and Pro subscribers see none. Data is used only to run the platform — authenticate you, run matches, compute rankings, and enforce per-tier quotas — and is not sold to third parties.
