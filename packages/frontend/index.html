TankMaze

A strategic programming game.

Somewhere in the labyrinth, an enemy tank is moving. You don't know where — only that your sensors just spiked and you have one tick to decide: advance, turn, fire, or wait for better data.

You don't drive a tank. You code one. Write the autonomous brain that drives your tank in Go, submit it, and watch your creation navigate fog-of-war, enemy contact, and the limits of its own hardware — without you.

What you can do

  • Brain — implement a Tick(sensors) Action function. It runs every 100 ms. Package-level variables are your tank's memory.
  • Loadout — distribute 15 points across speed, sensor range, damage, armor, and fire rate. Every build is a trade-off.
  • Game Days — round-robin groups of 8 followed by single-elimination. Placement awards global ranking points. Browse Game Days.
  • Replay — every match recorded tick-by-tick. Replay at any speed, inspect sensor readings, memory, and console output.

See how tanks stack up on the global leaderboard, or read the full help guide for stats, scoring, and match rules.
